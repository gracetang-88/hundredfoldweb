## Protect the Business You Built

Whether you run a restaurant, a retail store, an office, or a contracting business, one accident or lawsuit can put everything at risk. We help business owners get the right protection — explained clearly, priced competitively, and serviced in English or Chinese.

### General Liability

The foundation of business insurance. Covers third-party bodily injury, property damage, and legal defense costs. Landlords and contracts almost always require it — we can issue your Certificate of Insurance quickly.

### Commercial Property

Protects your building, equipment, inventory, and improvements against fire, theft, water damage, and more. Often combined with liability in a cost-effective Business Owner's Policy (BOP).

### Workers' Compensation

Required by California law for businesses with employees. We work with many carriers — including options for hard-to-place industries like restaurants and construction — to find competitive rates and help you through payroll audits.

### Get a Quote Online

For many common business types, you can **request a quote online in minutes**. Tell us about your business, and we will shop the market and come back with options — usually within one business day.
