## Retirement Income You Can Count On

After decades of saving, the question changes from "how do I grow my money?" to "how do I make it last?" Annuities are designed to answer exactly that — providing safe growth and income that is guaranteed for life, no matter how long you live or what the market does.

### Fixed Annuities (MYGA)

A multi-year guaranteed annuity works like a CD from an insurance company — a guaranteed interest rate locked in for a set number of years, often higher than bank CDs, with tax-deferred growth.

### Fixed Indexed Annuities (FIA)

Earn interest linked to a market index when the market goes up, while your principal is fully protected when the market goes down. A popular middle ground between safety and growth for retirement savings.

### Guaranteed Lifetime Income

Add an income rider and turn your savings into a "personal pension" — a paycheck that arrives every month for as long as you live. We help you compare income riders across carriers, because payout rates vary significantly.

### Rolling Over a 401(k) or IRA?

If you are retiring or changing jobs, we can help you move your retirement account into an annuity **without triggering taxes**, and coordinate the strategy with our tax team so your withdrawals are planned efficiently.
