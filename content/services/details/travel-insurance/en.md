## Insurance for Every Journey

A single emergency room visit in the U.S. can cost thousands of dollars — and visitors are not covered by domestic health plans. Whether your parents are visiting from overseas, you are traveling abroad, or you are planning the trip of a lifetime, the right travel coverage turns a potential disaster into a manageable bump in the road.

### Visitors to USA Insurance

Our most requested plan. If your parents or relatives are visiting the U.S., a visitor medical plan covers emergencies, hospital stays, and doctor visits during their stay. Options are available for older travelers and for certain pre-existing conditions.

### Travel Medical Insurance

Traveling outside the U.S.? Travel medical plans cover emergency care, evacuation, and repatriation worldwide — essential for international trips where your regular insurance does not follow you.

### Trip Protection

Protect your non-refundable trip costs against cancellation, interruption, and delays.
